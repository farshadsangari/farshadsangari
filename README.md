I’m **Farshad Sangari Abiz**, an **AI Software Engineer** and **Generative AI Researcher** with a background in  
**Computer Vision**, **Multimodal Learning**, and **Trustworthy AI**.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- 🧠 **Research** — interpretable & disentangled representations, generative models, multimodal learning, hallucination reduction
- ⚙️ **Engineering** — building **LLM- and VLM-powered agents**, APIs, and pipelines that are robust, reliable, observable, and scalable  
- 🧩 **Product** — using AI to actually solve problems and ship real value, not just benchmarks

I care a lot about **clean abstractions**, **reliable behavior in production**, and **systems that age well** as models, data, and requirements evolve.

---

### 🔭 What I’m Focused On

- **Next-Gen AI Agents**
  - Tool-using LLM agents (search, RAG, recommendation, analytics)
  - Multi-step reasoning, planning, and workflow automation
  - Evaluation setups for robustness, hallucination control, and latency–quality trade-offs

- **Generative & Multimodal AI**
  - Generative models for vision and representation learning (VAEs, diffusion, GAN-style architectures)
  - Multimodal retrieval and ranking (vision–language, text–image, hybrid signals)
  - Using interpretable latent spaces to make models more **explainable** and **trustworthy**

- **Smart, Scalable Systems**
  - Designing services that can be monitored, tested, and iterated on quickly
  - Clear interfaces between models, data, and business logic
  - Practical MLOps: experiment tracking, versioning, deployment best practices
